Introduction to Pharmacology

Pharmacology is the scientific study of drugs and their interactions with living organisms. Nursing pharmacology focuses on understanding how medications work, why they are prescribed, how they are administered safely, and how clients should be monitored for therapeutic effects and adverse reactions.

Learning Objectives

  • Define pharmacology and related terminology.
  • Explain how medications affect the human body.
  • Describe important principles of medication safety.
  • Recognize common adverse drug reactions.
  • Understand the nurse's responsibilities when administering medications.

Why Pharmacology Matters in Nursing

Nurses administer medications, monitor therapeutic responses, identify adverse effects, educate clients, and communicate important medication-related information to the healthcare team. A strong understanding of pharmacology therefore contributes directly to safe patient care.
